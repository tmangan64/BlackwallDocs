# Frontier: SciFi TTRPG

Frontier is the story of Humanity's final stand in a losing war against ancient alien forces.

In the early 1980s, researchers identified a group of massive objects approaching Sol following a trajectory that indicated steering, decision-making and intelligence. It was decided that these objects would be intercepted by a probe intended on establishing communications with the foreign fleet.

The probe (named Terran - 'from Earth') was launched in 1989 and reached the fleet approximately 40k AU from the Solar system's border where communication was immediately lost. It was determined that the probe had successfully reached its target and was intentionally destroyed by the approaching fleet.

The attack began with the total obliteration of all orbital satellites and culminated in a ground invasion beginning across multiple continents. Military assets were destroyed and entire navies were overrun. In a desperate effort, nuclear weapons were deployed against the enemy, reducing entire countries to irradiated wastelands. This proved enough to temper the enemy's shock arrival and bought enough time for a new category of weapon to be developed, the Sentinel.

## Sentinels
